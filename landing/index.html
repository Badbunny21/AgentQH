The problem

Your agent works in a chat app. You only see the last message.

Buried

Its updates get lost under everyone else's texts.

In the dark

You can't tell if it's working, stuck, or waiting on you.

Silent breaks

It can break quietly and you won't know until it's too late.

Made for small business owners and solo founders who run AI agents but don't have time to babysit them.

How it works

Three steps. No new brain.

01Connect your agent

Link the agent you already run on Telegram or Discord. It stays right where it is.

02Send a command

Tap out what you need from your phone. AgentHQ passes it straight to your agent.

03Watch it happen

Your agent says it got the command, reports progress, and finishes or fails where you can see it, with a full record.

Questions

Does AgentHQ use AI to answer for my agent?

No. Your agent does all the thinking on its own platform. AgentHQ only shows you what it's doing and passes along your commands.

Which agents can I connect?

Telegram and Discord agents to start, with more platforms later.

When can I try it?

Soon. AgentHQ is still being built by a small team, iPhone first. We'll share more here as it gets closer.
